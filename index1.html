Colorado Tarantula Migration

Each year in September and October, thousands of male Texas Brown Tarantulas leave their dens in search of mates across the American Southwest. In the state of Colorado, the Comanche National Grasslands are one of the best places to witness the mass migration, drawing thousands of tourists every year. Unfortunately, increased vehicle traffic on roads within the grasslands contributes to high mortality for male tarantulas. This map shows locations of tarantula sightings overlayed with highway and road networks in the grasslands to highlight sensitivity of the tarantulas to heightened vehicle traffic.
